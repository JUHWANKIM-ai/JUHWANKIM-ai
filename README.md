### Hi, I'm Juhwan Kim 👋

*Water the flowers, Pray for a garden.*

I'm a graduate student at the [FinX Lab](https://finx.hanyang.ac.kr/members/students), Hanyang University (Seoul),
working on **deep generative models for finance**: diffusion models for portfolio decisions and financial scenario
generation, deep hedging, and volatility forecasting.

**Research interests**
- Diffusion models (continuous and discrete) for returns, risk and portfolio allocation
- Text-paired financial time series: news, filings and prices
- Deep hedging and derivative pricing with neural operators
- Volatility forecasting and market microstructure
